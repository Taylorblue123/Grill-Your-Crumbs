# Product

## Register

product

## Users

People applying for jobs or opportunities who have useful experience in resumes, repositories, notes, portfolios, social posts, or diaries, but struggle to turn that material into specific and credible career stories. The first demo is used live by the founding team in front of startup-school peers and mentors.

## Product Purpose

Grill Your Crumbs reads a rough experience, shows what it understands, asks a short sequence of high-value follow-up questions, and produces resume material with inspectable provenance. Success is visible in one screen: the result contains valuable facts absent from the baseline, every factual segment can be traced to an existing source or a user's answer, and users copy or export the result.

## Brand Personality

Inquisitive, candid, and precise. The product should feel like a sharp editor who exposes its reasoning, gives the user control, and treats uncertainty honestly.

## Anti-references

- A generic ChatGPT clone with an undifferentiated message stream.
- A polished enterprise dashboard that hides the core before-and-after proof behind navigation and metrics.
- A resume generator that silently invents facts or labels model inference as user evidence.
- A crowded three-column climax that becomes illegible on a shared screen.

## Design Principles

- Show the evidence: question rationale and provenance must be visible in the interaction, not explained in a pitch.
- Slow production, fast consumption: support a real Live flow and a compact Replay flow for demos and sharing.
- Make correction productive: misunderstanding, weak questions, and unsupported claims each have an explicit recovery action.
- Earn every question: ask one focused question at a time, explain why it matters, and let the user skip or flag it.
- End on the proof: give the before-and-after artifact the full viewport and make newly uncovered facts unmistakable.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Provenance cannot depend on color alone; every state also carries a text label and icon or pattern. Support keyboard navigation, visible focus, readable projected type, high contrast, responsive layouts, and reduced-motion preferences.
